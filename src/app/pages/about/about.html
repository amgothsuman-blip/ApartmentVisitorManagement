ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Apartment Visitor Management System

A web-based application designed to simplify visitor registration, approval, and entry and exit tracking in apartment communities.

🎯

Our Objective

To organize visitor information and make apartment visitor management easier for residents and security staff.

🔐

Security Management

Provide a clear workflow for approving visitors and recording their arrival and departure.

📊

Visitor Reports

Display visitor statistics and allow visitor records to be exported for reporting purposes.

Technologies Used

Angular TypeScript HTML5 CSS3 Node.js Express.js

Developed as an Essential Web Programming project.
